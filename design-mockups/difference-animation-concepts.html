3 Ways to Animate the Comparison

Same content, same palette (paper/cream, olive-green for "us", red-soft for "them"), three different interaction models. Scroll down — concepts 2 & 3 replay when they enter view.

Concept 1

Drag-to-Reveal Slider

A literal before/after divider. Users drag the VS badge to physically uncover how much better the "real essays" side is — makes the comparison tactile instead of just read.

Spotlight + Marker Reveal
